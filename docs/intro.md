# Welcome to Physical AI & Humanoid Robotics

Welcome to the comprehensive textbook on **Physical AI & Humanoid Robotics**! This AI-native learning platform is designed to take you from foundational concepts to advanced practical implementations in the exciting field of embodied artificial intelligence.

## What You'll Learn

This textbook is organized into five comprehensive parts:

### 🎯 Part 1: Foundations of Physical AI
Understanding the intersection of artificial intelligence and physical systems, including the history, key concepts, and foundational principles.

### 🤖 Part 2: Robotics Fundamentals
Core robotics concepts including kinematics, dynamics, sensors, actuators, and control systems.

### 🚶 Part 3: Humanoid Robotics
Specialized topics in humanoid robot design, bipedal locomotion, manipulation, and human-robot interaction.

### 🧠 Part 4: AI for Robotics
Machine learning, computer vision, reinforcement learning, and modern AI techniques applied to robotics.

### 🛠️ Part 5: Practical Projects
Hands-on projects to implement what you've learned, from simple robots to advanced humanoid systems.

## Features

This is not just a static textbook. It includes:

- **AI Chatbot Assistant**: Ask questions about any topic and get instant answers with citations
- **Personalized Content**: Content adapts to your background and learning goals
- **Translation**: Read chapters in Urdu (or English) with proper formatting
- **Interactive Examples**: Code samples, simulations, and practical exercises
